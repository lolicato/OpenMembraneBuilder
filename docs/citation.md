## Related Software

OpenMembraneBuilder builds on several outstanding open-source scientific projects:

- [COBY](https://github.com/MikkelDA/COBY) – builds the systems
- [martinize2 / vermouth](https://github.com/marrink-lab/vermouth-martinize) – coarse-grains the proteins
- [Martini force field](http://cgmartini.nl)
- [MDAnalysis](https://www.mdanalysis.org) and [MDTraj](https://www.mdtraj.org)
- [Streamlit](https://streamlit.io) and [Mol*](https://molstar.org)
- NumPy, SciPy, pandas

Please also cite these projects where appropriate.
